# Data Use Policy

This policy applies to data accessed, processed, generated, stored, or transmitted through Zenyeno software, APIs, connectors, reports, or services.

## No data access rights are granted

Access to this source code does not grant permission to access any marketplace account, seller account, customer record, credential, private API, database, report, or third-party dataset. Users must have explicit authorization from the relevant data owner and must comply with the applicable platform terms and laws.

## Prohibited conduct

You may not use this project to:

- access an account, API, system, or dataset without authorization;
- bypass authentication, authorization, rate limits, or technical controls;
- scrape, collect, export, exfiltrate, copy, sell, or redistribute data without the data owner's permission;
- obtain or expose passwords, API keys, tokens, cookies, personal data, payment data, order data, or confidential business information;
- re-identify anonymized data or combine datasets to identify individuals without authorization;
- retain data longer than the authorized purpose requires;
- use collected data for an undisclosed purpose or train models without the required rights and consent.

## Security requirements

Authorized users must:

- use least-privilege credentials and keep secrets outside source control;
- encrypt sensitive data in transit and at rest;
- log and review access to sensitive systems;
- minimize collection and delete data when it is no longer required;
- immediately revoke exposed credentials and report suspected unauthorized access;
- comply with marketplace rules, privacy obligations, and data-processing agreements.

## Repository data

Production credentials, customer data, order exports, seller reports, and private datasets must never be committed to this repository. Only synthetic or properly anonymized examples may be published.

## Enforcement

Unauthorized data access or extraction is outside the permissions granted for this project. Access may be revoked, and violations may be reported to the affected platform, data owner, or relevant authority.
